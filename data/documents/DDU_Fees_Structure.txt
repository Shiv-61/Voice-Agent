Dharmsinh Desai University (DDU), Faculty of Technology
M.Tech Tuition Fee Breakdown & Payment Guidelines

1. M.Tech Program Fee Structure:
- First Year M.Tech (Admission Year 2023-24): Rs. 55,125 per annum
  (Fifty-Five Thousand One Hundred Twenty-Five Rupees)
- Second Year M.Tech (Admission Year 2022-23): Rs. 52,500 per annum
  (Fifty-Two Thousand Five Hundred Rupees)
- Third & Fourth Year: Not Applicable (-) as M.Tech is a 2-year postgraduate program.

2. Admission & Payment Rules:
- Fees are payable on an annual basis prior to semester commencement.
- Mode of payment: Online via Student Portal (Netbanking / UPI / Debit / Credit Cards)
  or offline via Bank Challan at designated bank counters.
- Application deadline for academic admission: 31 July 2026.
- For queries contact University Accounts Office: 0268-2520502 or info@ddu.ac.in.
